Multifunctional firmware for pentesting and everyday use.
- Wi-Fi: scanner, deauth, WPA bruteforce, handshake capture, evil twin, evil portal, web server, channel analyzer, weather, time sync, wifi info, wifi connect, password store
- Bluetooth: scanner, sniffer, bad BLE, air mouse, keyboard, presenter, camera shutter, bluetooth scan, device info, device finder, mouse jiggler.
- IR: read and save signal, send signal, TV-B-Gone.
- NFC (PN-532 & RC522): read and save cards, write 4-byte UID or sectors.
- USB: BAD USB, mouse jiggler, air mouse, keyboard, mass storage mode, CH9329 module support
- Files: edit, create, delete, rename files/folders, view images.
- UART terminal: recieving and sending text. baud rate and pins settings
- Settings: time, timezone, rotation, colors, fonts, brightness, status bar, volume, startup sound, menu items, language (12 languages) etc.
- Other: clock, battery, level tool, flashlight, sound level, system info
- 12 Languages: English, French, German, Portuguese, Turkish, Italian, Spanish, Indonesian, Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, Romanian.
- Modules: PN532, RC522, CH9329, SD, IR reciever.
